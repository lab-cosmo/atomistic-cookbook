Electron densities and electrostatic potentials with PET-ρ-OMol25
=================================================================

This recipe predicts the electron density of molecules with PET-ρ-OMol25, shows it
on planes through iodobenzene, and computes from it the electrostatic potential on the
molecular surface, from the σ-hole of iodobenzene to a series of 20 iodobenzenes and to
vitamin B12. It also predicts the spin density of two radicals and of a copper complex.
