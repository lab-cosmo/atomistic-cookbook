Computing the pKw of water with a machine-learning potential
============================================================

A step-by-step reproduction guide for the calculation of the autoionization
constant of water from atomistic simulations with a fine-tuned PET-MAD
potential: a smooth order parameter for the hydronium-hydroxide separation,
well-tempered metadynamics, fixed-bias replica exchange, reweighting,
calibration against the periodic Coulomb interaction, and nuclear quantum
corrections by mass thermodynamic integration. A template for the acidic and
basic dissociation of phosphoric acid species is included.
